Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026. Backsy is a prototype, and this policy describes what the prototype actually does — not what a finished product might do.

What we collect

  • Account name — the name you type to identify yourself in the app.
  • Transfers — sender, recipient, amount, status, and timestamps for each transfer you create.
  • Standard server logs — request time and path, kept only to keep the service running.

What we don't collect

No email address, no phone number, no payment details, no private keys. Backsy has no sign-up, no password, and no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Cookies

Backsy sets no tracking cookies. Your chosen name and your cookie-banner choice are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device, and are never sent anywhere.

Who we share it with

Nobody. Data is not sold, rented, or passed to advertisers. It is shared only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Transfer records are kept while the prototype is running so balances add up. Because this is a prototype, the database may be reset without notice — do not treat it as durable storage.

Your rights

Email hello@backsy.cc to ask what is held about you or to have it deleted. Since accounts carry no verified identity, deletion requests are handled by account name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Backsy runs on Solana devnet, a test network. The program is deployed and transfers really happen on-chain, but devnet coins have no value and the program has not been audited. Do not treat it as a place for real money.
