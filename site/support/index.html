Support

Need a hand?

Email hello@backsy.cc and we'll get back to you. The questions below cover most of what people ask first.

I sent a protected transfer. How does the other person get it?

Every protected transfer produces a claim link. Send that link to the recipient however you like. When they open it, the money moves to them and the transfer becomes final.

Can I take a transfer back?

Yes, any time before it's claimed. Open the transfer and hit Cancel — the full amount is back in your balance immediately. Once the recipient has claimed it, it's theirs and there is no way to reverse it.

Is the claim link safe to share?

Treat it like cash. Anyone who has the link can claim that transfer, so send it through a channel you trust and don't post it publicly. It cannot be guessed, but it can be forwarded.

What if nobody ever claims it?

After 7 days the transfer returns to your balance automatically. You don't have to remember to cancel it.

I sent to the wrong person. What now?

If it's still pending, cancel it — that's the whole point of Backsy. If they already claimed it, the money is gone, exactly as it would be in any other wallet.

Which networks are supported?

None yet. The current build runs on a simulated ledger. Solana is the first planned target for the on-chain version.

Has the code been audited?

No. Backsy is an early prototype and has had no external security review. Please don't put real money behind it in this form.

Where is my balance stored?

In the prototype, on the server, in a plain database. There is no wallet and no key on your device yet — that changes only when the on-chain version ships.

Backsy is an early prototype. Reversible transfers are demonstrated with a simulated escrow — no on-chain program is deployed and no real funds move.
